About

I am a Predoctoral Research Fellow at The Normal Lab at the University of Chicago and a master's student in economics at FGV EESP. My research is motivated by global challenges such as climate change and geoeconomic fragmentation: how people, markets, and institutions respond to them, and how we can measure those responses credibly. That agenda draws on econometric theory, where I work on inference under complex dependence, on behavioral and experimental economics, and on applications in development, environmental, and trade economics. I previously coordinated the academic team of OBECON, Brazil's national economics olympiad, and won individual gold medals at the International Economics Olympiad.
