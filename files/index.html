About

I am a master's student at FGV EESP finishing my degree before joining the University of Chicago as a Predoctoral Research Fellow in Fall 2026. My research interests lie at the intersection of econometric theory and applied economics. On the methods side, I am drawn to inference problems in panel and spatial settings — particularly how permutation-based methods can be made valid under dependence structures common in applied work. On the applied side, I am broadly interested in international trade, health economics, and behavioral economics. I also coordinate the academic team of OBECON, Brazil's national economics olympiad, and have represented Brazil at the International Economics Olympiad, where I won individual gold medals and helped lead the team to multiple championships.
