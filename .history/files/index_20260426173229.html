About

I am a master's student at FGV EESP finishing my degree before joining the University of Chicago as a Predoctoral Research Fellow in Summer 2026. My research interests lie at the intersection of econometric theory and applied economics. On the methods side, I work on inference problems in settings with complex dependence structures, such as panel and spatial data. On the applied side, I am broadly interested in international trade, health economics, and behavioral economics. I also coordinate the academic team of OBECON, Brazil's national economics olympiad, and have represented Brazil at the International Economics Olympiad, where I won individual gold medals and helped lead the team to multiple championships.
